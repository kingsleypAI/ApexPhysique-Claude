Magnesium is most effective taken at night. Bisglycinate and taurate forms are the most bioavailable and the most calming — they cross the blood-brain barrier and directly improve sleep depth.
Why it matters for this programme
▸ Sleep & recovery — Magnesium regulates melatonin and GABA, the two main sleep-onset chemicals. Poor sleep = high cortisol = stubborn belly fat. This is your sleep fix.
▸ Muscle cramps & soreness — Training 6 days/week depletes magnesium rapidly through sweat. Deficiency causes muscle cramps, spasms and delayed soreness. Restores electrolyte balance.
▸ Protein synthesis (Leucine) — The added 90mg leucine directly triggers the mTOR pathway, switching on muscle protein synthesis. Small dose but meaningful when taken consistently.
▸ Cortisol reduction — Magnesium taurate specifically blunts cortisol response. Lower cortisol = more fat mobilisation from stubborn areas (belly, lower back).

①
Morning — 1 tablet with breakfast
Fat-soluble vitamin — must be taken with food containing fat for absorption. Stack it with your Omega 3 morning capsule. Same meal, two supplements.
Especially important for you. You walk to the gym at early hours before sunrise. You work indoors. The UK has minimal UVB between October–April even at midday. You are almost certainly deficient — most UK adults are. Deficiency at your training intensity causes measurable drops in testosterone, strength output and fat metabolism.
Why it matters for this programme
▸ Testosterone production — D3 is a direct precursor to testosterone synthesis. Studies show men supplementing D3 see 20–25% increases in free testosterone. Critical for muscle gain and fat loss simultaneously.
▸ Strength & muscle function — Vitamin D receptors exist in muscle tissue. Deficiency directly reduces strength output and muscle contraction efficiency — you lift less than you should.
▸ Fat metabolism — D3 regulates genes involved in fat cell formation and breakdown. Low D3 is independently associated with higher body fat and slower fat loss response.
▸ Mood & motivation — D3 regulates serotonin. Low D3 = low mood, low drive, harder to get up early for gym sessions. Particularly relevant given your 4–6am starts.
▸ Bone & joint health — Heavy compound lifting (squats, bench, rows) under D3 deficiency accelerates joint wear. D3 maintains calcium absorption and bone mineral density.

The scale is a liar. You're doing a recomp — swapping fat for muscle. Some weeks it won't move or will go up. Judge by the mirror, your lifts, and how clothes fit.

Wk 6–10
Vascularity starts showing
▼

Veins become visible when two things happen together: body fat drops low enough that veins sit close to the skin, and muscle fullness pushes them outward. Arm veins typically appear around 14–15% body fat — you're close.

At 101kg down from 110kg you're already past the point where most people start seeing it in their forearms and biceps. The pump exercises in your programme (lateral raises, curls, cable fly) are the specific movements that dilate veins and make them more permanent over time.

Three things that directly increase vascularity: stay hydrated (dehydration collapses veins), keep sodium moderate (water retention masks veins), and the progressive overload on your curls and lateral raises — higher rep pump work is the most vascular-specific training.

Stay hydratedHigh rep pump workSodium moderate
Wk 8–12
Belly & stubborn fat shifts
▼

Belly and lower back fat are the last to go for men — this is biology, not failure. The fat cells here have more alpha-2 receptors that actively resist mobilisation. By week 8 the deficit has been consistent long enough to start clearing this layer.

You'll notice it first when relaxed — the belly starts sitting flatter instead of pushing out. Upper abs become visible when relaxed around week 10. Lower belly and hip fat is a week 12+ result.

Three things that accelerate this: 7+ hours sleep (cortisol destroys belly fat progress), no alcohol (suppresses fat oxidation for 36hrs), and your heavy compound lifts (squats/deadlifts spike testosterone and GH for hours after).

Sleep 7+ hrsAvoid alcoholCompounds are key

🥤 Easiest Win Available
Monster Original → Monster Zero Sugar
3 cans/day of Original is ~630 kcal and 162g sugar — daily. Switching to Zero Sugar keeps the exact same routine and caffeine but drops that to ~30–45 kcal. That's roughly 590 kcal/day recovered without changing anything else about your food.
Variety Fixes Food Fatigue
Eating the same thing every day is the fastest way to fall off a cut — not because the food is wrong, but because you stop wanting to eat it. Everything below is grouped by meal slot with several swappable options. Pick whichever sounds good that day — all options in a group are roughly matched for protein and calories.
